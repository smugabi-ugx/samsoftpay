The service

We provide payment collection (MTN Mobile Money and crypto) and disbursement services for merchants in Uganda, through an API, hosted checkout pages and a merchant dashboard. Airtel Money and card payments are in development and are not yet available for live transactions.

Fees

Collections: 1.5% per successful charge, minimum UGX 200, capped at UGX 5,000. Payouts/disbursements: the same 1.5% (min UGX 200, cap UGX 5,000), no flat fee. No monthly or setup fees, and no charges beyond applicable URA taxation. Fees are deducted from the collected amount before settlement; the fee schedule in force at the time of a transaction applies to that transaction.

Settlement

Collected funds are held for a 24-hour settlement hold after a successful charge, then become available for withdrawal. Every transaction is recorded on a double-entry ledger and reconciled against the mobile-money network's own records.

Refunds

Refunds are initiated by the merchant (via the dashboard or API) and return the net collected amount to the customer's mobile-money account. The original processing fee is not returned.
